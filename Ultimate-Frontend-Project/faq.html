Common questions

Everything you need to know.

If you cannot find the answer you are looking for, feel free to contact our team.

What services does TechNova provide?

TechNova provides web development, UI/UX design, data and analytics solutions, cloud planning and digital product development for growing businesses.

How do you start a new project?

We begin with a short discussion about your goals, requirements, target audience and expected timeline. After understanding the project, we define the scope and recommend the right approach.

How long does a typical website project take?

Project timelines depend on the size and complexity of the website. A small business website may take a few weeks, while larger projects can require additional planning and development.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can review an existing website and improve its visual design, structure, usability, accessibility and responsive experience while keeping the business goals in focus.

Do you create responsive websites?

Yes. Our websites are designed to provide a consistent experience across desktops, tablets and mobile devices.

Can I request changes during the project?

Yes. Feedback and revisions are an important part of the design and development process. Changes can be discussed and included according to the agreed project scope.

Do you provide website maintenance and support?

Yes. Ongoing support can include content updates, design improvements, performance checks and general website maintenance.

How can I get started?

The easiest way to get started is to contact us with a short description of your project. Our team can then discuss your requirements and the next steps.
